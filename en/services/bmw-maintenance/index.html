Thoughtful preventive care for confident everyday driving

BMW service requirements vary by model, mileage, age and driving conditions. At AAB in Lat Krabang, Bangkok, you can discuss scheduled maintenance and vehicle-condition checks with a team familiar with BMW service needs. We recommend reviewing your vehicle details and service history before choosing any work.

What we can discuss

01 / 04

Fluid and oil service

The correct fluids and service intervals depend on the specific model and manufacturer requirements.

02 / 04

Brakes and tyres

Brake wear, tyre condition and safety-related components deserve regular inspection.

03 / 04

Cooling system and leak checks

Visible leaks and abnormal operating temperatures may call for targeted diagnosis.

04 / 04

Maintenance history review

Mileage, service records and dashboard alerts help define a sensible inspection scope.

Frequently asked questions

How often should a BMW be serviced?

Intervals vary by model, engine, driving conditions and the vehicle’s service indicator. Consult the owner’s manual and assess the actual vehicle condition.

Can I ask about maintenance for an older BMW?

Yes. Please share the model and maintenance history so the team can assess the appropriate service discussion.

Is there a fixed price for every BMW?

No. Prices vary by model, fluids, parts and required work. Contact AAB with your vehicle details for an assessment.

Actual work and costs depend on the vehicle inspection. This is general information, not a diagnosis of your particular car.
