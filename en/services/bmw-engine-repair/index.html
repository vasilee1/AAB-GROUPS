Identify the cause before choosing a repair

A rough idle, loss of power, warning light or fluid leak can have multiple causes. Replacing parts based only on a symptom can be costly. A structured approach starts with a clear description of the fault, an appropriate visual inspection and diagnostic information where useful.

What we can discuss

01 / 04

Rough idle or vibration

Note whether the problem occurs on a cold start, under load or continuously.

02 / 04

Loss of power or hesitation

The conditions under which the symptom appears help narrow the possible systems involved.

03 / 04

Oil or coolant leaks

Leak location and vehicle condition help determine how urgently inspection is needed.

04 / 04

Check-engine warning

Diagnostic codes are clues that require interpretation and confirmation, not automatic part-replacement instructions.

Preparing for your visit

01

Describe the symptoms

Share the vehicle model, year, warning messages and when the issue occurs.

02

Arrange diagnosis

The team can review relevant vehicle systems and diagnostic findings.

03

Confirm next steps

Discuss the proposed scope, estimated cost and options before repairs begin.

Frequently asked questions

Does a rough-running BMW always need new ignition coils?

No. Ignition, fuel, intake and other engine systems may produce similar symptoms. Diagnosis should come before replacing parts.

Can I drive with the check-engine light on?

If it flashes or the vehicle runs very poorly, overheats or loses power, stop safely and seek assistance before continuing.

Can I send a video of the fault?

Yes. Video and warning-screen photos can help the initial discussion, although the cause may require an in-person inspection.

Actual work and costs depend on the vehicle inspection. This is general information, not a diagnosis of your particular car.
